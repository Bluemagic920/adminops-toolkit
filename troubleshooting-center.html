    Common Windows and IT troubleshooting tasks

    Use the guided checklist to collect evidence before making changes, or choose the command builder for read-only PowerShell diagnostics. The tools on this page generate instructions and text; they do not connect to or modify your devices.

    Windows 11 update errors and servicing

    Start by recording the failed KB number and exact Windows Update error. Check disk space, system time, update service status, and recent WindowsUpdateClient events before attempting repairs. The error lookup includes 0x80070005, 0x80070643, and 0x800F081F.

    Intune and SCCM / ConfigMgr troubleshooting

    For enrollment or compliance issues, review dsregcmd /status, relevant MDM events, and device management assignments. For ConfigMgr client problems, check the CcmExec service and client logs such as LocationServices.log and AppEnforce.log. For a dedicated workflow, see the Intune Diagnostics Center or SCCM Query Builder.

    DNS, network and Microsoft 365 access

    Compare DNS resolution and TCP port connectivity with the application's expected endpoints. For Microsoft 365 sign-in issues, capture timestamps and correlation IDs, and check service health and Conditional Access using authorized administrative access.

    These checks are starting points, not guaranteed fixes. Avoid changing enterprise configurations without approval and a recovery plan.
