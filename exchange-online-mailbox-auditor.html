Exchange Online Mailbox Auditor

Generate read-only PowerShell reports for shared mailbox permissions, delegates, calendar access, and mailbox configuration.

Scripts require the ExchangeOnlineManagement module, a Microsoft 365 sign-in, and appropriate permissions. Review before running. CSV reports can contain sensitive organizational information. No credentials or mailbox data are sent to this page.

Read-only mailbox auditing; no mailbox permissions are modified. This tool generates scripts but does not run them. Large-tenant reports can take time and may be subject to Exchange Online throttling.
