Frequently asked questions

How do I create an account?

Open the homepage, select Account, and follow the sign-up instructions. Confirm your email if requested, then sign in.

Where are the free PowerShell scripts?

Open the homepage and choose Free scripts. Browse and review the scripts before running them.

Where are the free IT applications?

Open IT Applications from the navigation to browse the available browser-based tools.

Where do I find the 30 Premium toolkits?

Open Member Dashboard to search, filter, and favorite toolkits. Use the protected catalog on the homepage to request downloads.

Why can’t I download a Premium ZIP?

Make sure you are signed in and that your membership status is Pro Active. Refresh your status, then use the protected download catalog. If the status cannot be verified, try again later.

My payment succeeded but my status is not Pro Active. What should I do?

Do not purchase again immediately. Refresh membership status, verify that you are signed in with the same email used for checkout, and contact the site operator with the approximate payment date. Never send your card number or password.

Can I run these scripts on production computers?

Review the code and test in a non-production environment first. Some administrative scripts require elevated permissions or may change system settings.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Use the billing portal link below. Use the email associated with your Stripe subscription; the billing portal is operated by Stripe.

Do favorites sync across devices?

No. Dashboard favorites are saved in the current browser and may disappear if you clear site data or switch browsers.

Is the dashboard itself a secure file storage area?

No. The dashboard is a toolkit directory. Protected download authorization is enforced by the backend, not by hiding dashboard content.
