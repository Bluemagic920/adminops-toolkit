Security notes
Recovery passwords grant access to encrypted data. This generator deliberately avoids commands that print, copy, or upload recovery passwords. Protector inventory only lists protector types and IDs. A listed protector or device join state does not prove that a recovery key was successfully backed up to Microsoft Entra ID or Active Directory.
For managed environments, verify recovery-key escrow through your approved Intune, Entra, or Active Directory administrative workflow. This tool does not contact those services or validate escrow remotely.
